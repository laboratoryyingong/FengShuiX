# Privacy Policy

**App Name:** Feng Shui Yi  
**Last Updated:** 31 Oct 2025  
**Contact:** max.gong.developer@hotmail.com

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how the app **“Feng Shui Yi”** (the “App”, “we”, “our”) handles your information. The App is a utility tool for feng shui inspection, orientation measurement, and 24-Mountain display. It is designed to run primarily **on your device** and does not actively collect your personal information or send it to our servers.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect
In the current version, the App **does not require you to create an account** and does **not** actively collect:

- Your name, email address, or phone number
- Your contacts, photos, or calendar data
- Your precise location
- Device identifiers used for advertising or cross-app tracking
- User-generated content

If future versions require additional data to provide new features, we will clearly inform you and, where required, obtain your consent.

## 3. Use of Device Sensors
To provide core features such as compass, direction alignment, and 24-Mountain positioning, the App may access certain device sensors (e.g. magnetometer, accelerometer, gyroscope).

- This processing happens **locally on your device**.
- Sensor readings are used only to calculate direction and display feng shui information.
- We **do not** upload, store, or share these sensor readings with third parties.

## 4. Network and Data Transmission
In its current version, the App works mainly offline and **does not transmit your personal data to our servers**.

If future versions add cloud backup, online content, or analytics, we will update this Policy and ask for your permission where necessary.

## 5. Third-Party Services
At this time:

- The App does **not** include advertising SDKs.
- The App does **not** include third-party analytics by default.
- The App does **not** sell or share your data with third parties.

If we later integrate optional third-party services (e.g. crash reporting, usage analytics), we will identify them here and describe what data they collect and why.

## 6. Data Storage and Security
Most processing is done locally on your device. We recommend that you:

- Download the App only from official app stores.
- Keep your device and the App updated.
- Protect your device with a passcode or biometric lock.

## 7. Children’s Privacy
The App is not specifically directed to children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Your Choices and Rights
Because we do not store your personal data on our servers, there is usually no online data for us to provide, correct, or delete. However, you may:

- Uninstall the App to remove local data.
- Contact us if you have questions about sensor usage or about future cloud features.

## 9. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or in applicable laws. The updated version will be published at the same URL. Your continued use of the App after such changes means you accept the updated Policy.
